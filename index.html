Why Miami hair needs keratin care

Miami is beautiful, but heat, sun, salt and humidity are hard on hair. Moisture in the air makes cuticles swell, which leads to frizz and puffiness. A Brazilian keratin treatment coats each strand with a smoothing protein layer that blocks much of that effect. The result is hair that dries faster, styles easier and keeps its shape on humid days.

Many guests who visit us for the first time say they spend far less time with a blow dryer and flat iron afterward. That is a real benefit for busy schedules, travel and active lifestyles.

Frequently asked questions

How long does a Brazilian keratin treatment last?

Most last two to five months, depending on hair type, wash habits and aftercare products.

How long does the appointment take?

Plan on about two to four hours depending on hair length and thickness.

Can I wash my hair after the treatment?

Your stylist will give you exact timing. Sulfate free shampoo is recommended afterward to protect the results.

Is keratin treatment good for damaged hair?

Yes, it helps reduce frizz and adds smoothness. For heavy damage we may suggest hair repair first.
